# Chatting with Oraya on WhatsApp — Guest Guide

*A simple guide to messaging Oraya. Written for guests — reuse it in website FAQs, Instagram highlights, WhatsApp support replies, booking emails, or sales scripts.*

*This guide is intentionally free of any technical detail. It describes only what a guest experiences.*

---

## Message us naturally

There is no menu to memorise. Just tell Oraya what you have in mind, in your own words — one message is enough. Our concierge will guide you the rest of the way and, when it helps, send you a secure link to continue on our website.

## Plan a private stay

Share your dates, your preferred villa, and how many guests — together or a few at a time. For example:

> *"I want Mechmech from July 10 to July 15 for 4 guests, 2 bedrooms."*

Oraya will read that back to you and offer a secure link to finish your request on our website. **A stay is a request until Oraya confirms availability** — we'll follow up to confirm.

## Plan an event or celebration

Tell us you'd like to celebrate — a birthday, a private dinner, an engagement, a family gathering. For example:

> *"I want to plan a birthday dinner."*

We'll send you straight to our event inquiry page, where you can choose your date, setup, and services and share the details. **An event inquiry is not a confirmed event booking** until the Oraya team has reviewed it and followed up.

## Explore the villas

Curious first? Just ask. For example:

> *"Can I see the villas?"*

We'll point you to Villa Mechmech and Villa Byblos so you can explore before you decide.

## Check an existing booking

Already booked with us and want an update? Message us. For example:

> *"Check my booking."*  ·  *"My reservation status."*

To protect your booking, we may ask for your **booking reference** (the short code from your confirmation) and, sometimes, the **email or full name** on the booking. Once we can recognise you, we'll share your safe booking details.

## What helps us respond faster

- Your **dates** and **preferred villa** for a stay.
- The **number of guests** (and bedrooms, if you know).
- The **occasion** for an event.
- Your **booking reference** when asking about an existing reservation.

## What the concierge can help with

- Starting a private-stay request and sending you a secure link to complete it.
- Guiding you to the right event inquiry page.
- Helping you explore the villas.
- Recognising your existing booking and sharing safe status details.

## What the Oraya team confirms personally

Some things are always handled by a real person on the Oraya team — never promised automatically:

- Whether your dates are **available** and your booking is **confirmed**.
- **Payment** of any kind.
- **Arrival details, access codes, and directions.**
- Any **special request or exception**.

We'll always follow up to take care of these with you directly. ✨
