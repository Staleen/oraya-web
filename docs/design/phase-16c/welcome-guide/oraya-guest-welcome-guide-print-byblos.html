Comfort & Using the Villa

Wi-Fi

Password Oraya2026

TV & Entertainment

The living room includes one Smart TV.

AC & Cooling

Each room has an air conditioner. The controller is mounted on the wall next to the light switch. The units support cooling and heating.

Hot Water

Hot water is prepared for your stay. The villa uses a solar-heated tank that heats during the day and maintains hot water through the night.

If any issue, contact Oraya before attempting a reset.

Generator & Power

The villa has a backup generator for power outages. It activates automatically when needed. You may hear it start briefly — this is normal and all systems remain on.

Water Supply

Water is supplied from the municipality and backed by a rooftop storage tank. If water pressure drops, the pump activates automatically. Contact Oraya if the issue persists.

If something does not work as expected, contact Oraya before attempting a fix.  +961 71 140 041

House Expectations

Visitors & Gatherings

You may host up to 20 visitors during your stay. If you would like to expand your stay into a small event or celebration, Oraya can assist with catering, decoration, music, and ambience.

Restricted Areas

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, security systems, security cameras, and storage rooms are restricted areas. Please do not access, adjust, or tamper with them.

Smoking

Smoking is allowed on the terrace and outdoor areas. Please use the ashtrays provided and dispose of cigarettes responsibly. Smoking is not permitted indoors.

Pool Safety

No diving in the pool. Children must be supervised near the pool at all times. Life rings are available poolside. Always replace the cover when finished.

Children & Supervision

Children and underage guests must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Property Care

Please treat the villa, furniture, equipment, and outdoor areas with care throughout your stay.

Quiet Hours

Please be considerate of neighbours. Keep music and conversations at a respectful volume, especially between 11:00 PM and 8:00 AM.

Keys & Security

Always lock doors and windows when leaving the villa. Secure the front gate behind you. Contact Oraya immediately if a key or access code is lost.
