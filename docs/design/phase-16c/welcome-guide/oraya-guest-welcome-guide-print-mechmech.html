TV & Entertainment

The living room includes one Smart TV. The winter room also includes a TV.

NETFLIX

Heating

During winter, the house heating system will be on before you arrive. The heating uses the same labeled switch area as the water-heater system. To reduce or turn off heating in a room, adjust the radiator valve in that room.

Hot Water

Hot water is available during your stay. It can be turned on from the labeled switch. During summer, turn on the labeled switch and allow approximately 15 minutes.

If any issue, contact Oraya before attempting a reset.

Garbage

Every room has a small bin. Two large cans are outside. Place all garbage in the outdoor cans — not on the street.

If something does not work as expected, contact Oraya before attempting a fix.  +961 71 140 041

House Expectations

Visitors & Gatherings

Host up to 20 visitors during your stay. For a small event or celebration, Oraya can arrange catering, décor, music, and ambience.

Restricted Areas

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, security, and storage areas are restricted. Please do not access, adjust, or tamper with them.

Smoking

Smoking is allowed on the terrace and outdoors — please use the ashtrays provided. Not permitted indoors.

Pool Safety

No diving. Children must be supervised near the pool at all times. Life rings are poolside. Replace the cover after use.

Children & Supervision

Children and underage guests must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Property Care

Please treat the villa, furniture, and outdoor areas with care throughout your stay.

Quiet Hours

Be considerate of neighbours — keep music and conversation low, especially between 11:00 PM and 8:00 AM.

Keys & Security

Lock doors and windows when leaving, and secure the gate behind you. Tell Oraya at once if a key or code is lost.
