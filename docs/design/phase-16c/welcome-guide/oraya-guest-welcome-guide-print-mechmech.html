Utilities & Comfort

Wi-Fi

Network ORAYA

Password Oraya2026

TV & Entertainment

The living room includes one Smart TV. The winter room also includes a TV.

Heating

During winter, the house heating system will be on before you arrive. The heating uses the same labeled switch area as the water-heater system. To reduce or turn off heating in a room, adjust the radiator valve in that room.

Hot Water

Hot water is available during your stay. It can be turned on from the labeled switch. During summer, turn on the labeled switch and allow approximately 15 minutes.

If any issue, contact Oraya before attempting a reset.

Kitchen

BBQ

Concept

Required: BBQ (Mechmech)

The outdoor BBQ is positioned outside the winter glass room, protected under the roof. It includes a barbecue kit. After use, make sure it is fully turned off.

Fireplace & Diesel Stove

Concept

Required: Fireplace (Mechmech)

The winter glass room includes a traditional Lebanese diesel stove heater, prepared with a full tank. Additional diesel may be charged separately. The stove also has an oven compartment and a top surface for warming food.

Use only as instructed. Do not move, refill, or leave unattended. Contact Oraya if you need help.

Winter Room

Concept

Required: Winter Room (Mechmech)
