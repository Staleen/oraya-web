Welcome

It is our pleasure to welcome you to Villa Byblos. We hope your time here is restful, private, and entirely effortless.

This little book holds everything you need for your stay — how to arrive and let yourself in, how the villa and its comforts work, the few things we kindly ask of our guests, and how to reach us at any hour.

Should you need anything at all — a recommendation, an extra comfort, or simply a question — our concierge is only a message away, day or night.

Until then, make yourself completely at home. We are so glad to have you.

— The Oraya Team

02 Chapter Two

The House

Wi-FiMesh coverage
Network  ORAYA Password  Oraya2026
TV & Entertainment
The living room includes one Smart TV. NETFLIX
AC & Cooling
Each room has an air conditioner. The controller is mounted on the wall next to the light switch. The units support cooling and heating.
Hot Water
Hot water is prepared for your stay. The villa uses a solar-heated tank that heats during the day and maintains hot water through the night. If any issue, contact Oraya before attempting a reset.
Generator & Power
The villa has a backup generator for power outages. It activates automatically when needed. You may hear it start briefly — this is normal and all systems remain on.
Water Supply
Water is supplied from the municipality and backed by a rooftop storage tank. If water pressure drops, the pump activates automatically. Contact Oraya if the issue persists.
If something does not work as expected, contact Oraya before attempting a fix.  +961 71 140 041

Chapter Two · continued

Living Spaces

Pool & Pool Cover

Pool is maintained before your stay. Remove the cover before entering and replace when finished. No diving. Contact Oraya for any pool issue before attempting a reset.

Kitchen

The kitchen is fully operational and includes a kettle, microwave, gas cooktop, stove, cutlery, and glassware.

Barbecue

Outdoor BBQ with barbecue kit. Open the gas valve and ignite as labeled. After use: close the valve, allow to cool. Do not leave unattended while lit. Clean the grate after each use.

Bathrooms

Towels and amenities are provided. Hot water is ready on arrival. Used towels can be left on the bathroom floor.

Garbage

Every room has a small bin. Two large cans are outside. Place all garbage in the outdoor cans — not on the street.

03 Chapter Three

House Notes

  1. i. Visitors & Gatherings Host up to 20 visitors during your stay. For a small event or celebration, Oraya can arrange catering, décor, music, and ambience.
  2. ii. Restricted Areas Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, security, and storage areas are restricted. Please do not access, adjust, or tamper with them.
  3. iii. Smoking Smoking is allowed on the terrace and outdoors — please use the ashtrays provided. Not permitted indoors.
  4. iv. Pool Safety No diving. Children must be supervised near the pool at all times. Life rings are poolside. Replace the cover after use.
  5. v. Children & Supervision Children and underage guests must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  6. vi. Property Care Please treat the villa, furniture, and outdoor areas with care throughout your stay.
  7. vii. Quiet Hours Be considerate of neighbours — keep music and conversation low, especially between 11:00 PM and 8:00 AM.
  8. viii. Keys & Security Lock doors and windows when leaving, and secure the gate behind you. Tell Oraya at once if a key or code is lost.
