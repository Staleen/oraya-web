### 1. `gate-driveway-concept.jpg`

**Used in:**
- Digital guide Step 3 — At the Gate
- Print Page 2 — Arrival & Access

**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (landscape) or 4:3

**Generation prompt:**
```
Luxury private villa entrance gate in Lebanon, stone boundary wall, modern black
sliding gate, warm exterior lighting at dusk or evening, discreet keypad area visible
on one side, clean driveway approach with stone or paved surface, high-end boutique
villa arrival atmosphere, realistic architectural photography style, no people, no cars,
no text, no logos, no branding.
```

**Negative prompt / avoid:**
- People, cars, pedestrians
- Generic suburban gate
- Visible PIN pads showing numbers
- Brand logos, street signs, license plates
- Daylight studio-style lighting (prefer warm evening)
- Low-quality or illustrative style

**Label requirement:** "Concept image" overlay, bottom-right
