Each room has an air conditioner. The controller is mounted on the wall next to the light switch. The units support cooling and heating.

Hot Water

Confirmed

Hot water is prepared for your stay. The villa uses a solar-heated tank that heats during the day and maintains hot water through the night. If you experience any issue with hot water, contact Oraya before attempting any reset.

Pool

Confirmed

The pool is available for guest use and is maintained before your stay. It is kept covered to help protect it from leaves and insects.

Remove the pool cover manually before use and place it back after you finish using the pool, to help protect the water from leaves and insects.

Kitchen & BBQ

Confirmed

The outdoor area includes a functional BBQ/kitchenette section with a barbecue kit. After using the BBQ, make sure it is fully turned off.

Heating

Confirmed

During winter, the house heating system will be on before you arrive. The heating uses the same labeled switch area as the water-heater system. If you want to reduce or turn off heating in a room, adjust the radiator valve in that room.

Fireplace & Diesel Stove

Confirmed

The winter glass room includes a traditional Lebanese diesel stove heater. It is prepared with a full tank. Additional diesel use may be charged separately. The stove also includes an oven compartment and a top surface that may be used for warming food.

Use only as instructed. Do not move the heater, refill it yourself, or leave it unattended. If you need help, contact Oraya.

Winter Room

Confirmed

The winter room is accessible from the kitchen or from outside. It has a panoramic glass view, a kitchenette, a TV, and lounge seating. It functions as an additional living area during winter, separate from the main indoor living room.

Hot Water

Confirmed

Hot water is available during your stay. It can be turned on or off from the labeled switch shown in the villa. During summer, if you need hot water, turn on the labeled switch and allow approximately 15 minutes.

Pool

Confirmed
Concept image

The Mechmech pool is covered. If you use the pool, remove the cover manually before use and place it back after you finish. If the pool is heated for your stay, replacing the cover helps retain heat. If the pool is not heated, replacing the cover is not required unless instructed by Oraya.

Kitchen & BBQ

Confirmed

The kitchen is fully operational and includes a kettle, microwave, gas cooktop, stove, cutlery, and glassware.

The outdoor BBQ is positioned outside the winter glass room while still protected under the roof, allowing it to be used comfortably during colder weather. The area includes a functional BBQ/kitchenette section with a barbecue kit. After using the BBQ, make sure it is fully turned off.
