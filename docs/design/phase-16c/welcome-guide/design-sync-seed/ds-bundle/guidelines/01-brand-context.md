# Oraya — Brand Context

Oraya is a luxury boutique villa brand in Lebanon. Two villas:

- **Villa Byblos** — Mastita, Jbeil (summer-leaning: pool, AC, solar hot water)
- **Villa Mechmech** — Mechmech, Sama (winter-leaning: heating, diesel stove, panoramic winter glass room)

Website: stayoraya.com · Concierge: +961 71 140 041 (WhatsApp/call, 24/7).

The brand promise: a stay that is **restful, private, and entirely effortless**, with a
concierge "only a message away, day or night."

## Palette (brand constants — keep)

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | `#C5A46D` | Primary accent — rules, ornaments, accents |
| Gold deep | `#B08D4F` | Stronger gold for fine linework/icons |
| Terracotta | `#B0714B` | Warm secondary accent (used sparingly) |
| Olive | `#7C7A54` | Tertiary warm accent (rare) |
| Beige | `#E7DECC` | Warm surface |
| Beige light | `#F3EBDC` | Lighter warm surface |
| Ivory | `#FAF6EE` | Near-white warm surface |
| Charcoal | `#2E2E2E` | Body text |
| Midnight | `#1F2B38` | Dark surface / display text — the brand's "night" |
| Muted | `#8a8070` | Secondary text, captions |

Alpha variants of gold (≈10% and ≈28%) are used for faint fills and soft hairlines.
White is the default page ground. Midnight-on-gold and gold-on-midnight are the
strongest brand pairings.

## Typography (brand constants — keep)

- **Playfair Display** — display only: titles, large numerals, italic accents.
- **Lato** — all body and functional copy. **This is an identity rule: body copy is
  never set in the serif.**
- Letterspaced uppercase Lato (wide tracking, small sizes) is the established
  label/eyebrow voice.

Type *scale, hierarchy, and composition are open to redesign* — only the pairing
and the body-copy rule are fixed.

## Motifs (available, not mandatory)

- The **arch** — arched tops on image plates and line-art arches echo Lebanese
  architecture. A recurring but optional device.
- Thin gold **hairlines** and a small rotated-square **gem ornament**.
- Full-bleed **midnight plates** as rhythm pages (cover / interludes / back cover).

New explorations may keep, evolve, or replace these motifs as long as the result
still reads unmistakably Oraya (palette + type + tone).

## Voice & tone

Warm, assured, unhurried, host-like — never corporate, never instructional-manual.
Short sentences. "We" is the Oraya team speaking to a guest by name. Operational
content must stay **scannable and unambiguous** even inside an editorial voice —
hard facts (times, numbers, credentials) always appear in a scannable form, never
buried in prose.

## Logo / wordmark

The wordmark is the word **ORAYA** in letterspaced uppercase. SVG emblem/logo
components exist in the website codebase (`OrayaEmblem`, `OrayaLogoFull`) and can be
requested if an exploration needs them; they are not included in this seed.
