# Current V2 — Honest Critique (what to move beyond)

The current V2 prototype ("The House Book", 12 pages, checkpointed in the repo)
rates ~**8.7/10** after one art-direction pass (from 7.4). It is a competent
editorial baseline — and that is exactly why it must not be copied: its ceiling
is visible. Target: **9/10 minimum** via genuinely new direction.

## What V2 got right (worth preserving as *principles*, not layouts)

- A page-2 "Essentials" dashboard — the five facts guests actually look up,
  findable in seconds. Keep the *principle*: essentials surface early and fast.
- Dark–light–dark rhythm (midnight cover → light content → midnight interlude →
  midnight back cover) gave the book a spine.
- Photo-independence: it looks intentional with zero villa photography.
- Operational clarity survived the editorial voice (checkout and emergency stayed
  list/panel form).

## Where V2 falls short — the reasons it is not the target

1. **Still partly manual-like.** Several spreads remain stacked full-width rows
   under a heading. The *voice* is editorial; the *bones* are still a document.
2. **Ledger/list monoculture.** Essentials ledger, systems ledger, medallion
   ledger, notes list, checklist, directory — six variations of the same
   label-left/text-right row pattern. By page 10 the rhythm is monotone.
   A 9/10 book needs compositional variety: scale contrast, asymmetry, spreads
   that breathe differently from one another.
3. **Art direction is conservative.** Ornament is limited to hairlines, one gem
   motif, and two thin arch line-drawings. There is no illustration language, no
   expressive use of scale, no page where the design takes a risk. It is tasteful
   and forgettable.
4. **Single-column monotony.** Almost every content page is one column at one
   text size on the same grid. No spread thinks as a *spread*.
5. **The medallion icons are generic.** Line icons in circles read "hotel
   directory," not "Oraya." A proprietary illustration/motif language would be
   distinctly stronger.
6. **Typographic timidity.** Playfair appears mostly at similar display sizes;
   the ghost chapter numerals are the only large-scale type moment. The serif's
   italic, optical sizes, and dramatic scale range are unexplored.
7. **Process lesson:** V2 was designed directly in HTML/CSS, which quietly
   constrained every decision toward what flexbox does easily. The next
   direction must be designed in Figma / Claude Design first, then implemented.

## Constraint reminders that shaped V2 (still true for any new direction)

- No real villa photography yet (see photo-slot rules in the exploration brief).
- A4 portrait; multiple-of-4 page count; both villas from one system.
- All facts fixed; hard facts must stay scannable; checkout/emergency stay
  high-clarity.
