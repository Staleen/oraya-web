# Design Exploration Brief — Oraya Welcome Book

## The task

Design **new professional directions** for a printed luxury villa welcome book.
This is an open exploration: new page composition, rhythm, grid, hierarchy, and
editorial systems. Keep the Oraya brand identity (`ORAYA_BRAND_CONTEXT.md`);
everything else is yours to reinvent.

**The current V2 prototype is a checkpoint, not the target. Do not copy its
layout.** Read `CURRENT_V2_CRITIQUE.md` for what it gets wrong; the new work
should clearly surpass it.

## Quality bar

- Minimum **9/10 professional visual direction** — the standard of a boutique
  hotel group's printed compendium or a fine editorial book, judged on: luxury
  feel, modernity, brand fit, print elegance, guest usefulness, and how far it
  escapes "instruction manual."
- Design first in **Figma / Claude Design**. HTML/CSS implementation happens only
  after a direction is approved — the design should lead the code, not inherit
  its constraints.

## Print constraints (hard)

- **A4 portrait** (210 × 297 mm), 14 mm nominal safety margins for content pages;
  full-bleed plates allowed (assume +3 mm bleed for professional print).
- Physical page count should be a **multiple of 4** (saddle-stitch); 12 or 16
  pages are the expected range. Page count within that is a design decision.
- Must survive both professional print and plain office A4 printing (no
  hairlines thinner than ~0.5 pt, no critical content in the outer 5 mm).
- One book per villa; Byblos first. The system must absorb the Mechmech content
  variance (different systems/spaces) without redesign.

## Content rules (hard)

- Content comes from `WELCOME_BOOK_CONTENT_BRIEF.md` — **content reference only,
  not a layout map**. Regroup and re-sequence freely; drop nothing.
- **Never invent** facts, amenities, services, photos, safety instructions, or
  emergency information.
- Hard facts (times, numbers, credentials, emergency numbers) must remain
  **scannable at a glance** wherever they land — a guest holding the book in a
  hallway must find the Wi-Fi or the checkout time in seconds.
- Emergency and checkout content must stay high-clarity: list/panel form is
  required there, however editorial the rest becomes.

## Photo-slot rules (hard)

- **Real villa photography does not exist yet** (only two real device photos:
  gate keypad, front-door lock). Renders and stock exist but misrepresent the
  real villas and should not drive the design.
- **No fake final photography** — no stock, no AI imagery presented as the villa.
- Design must be **excellent before photos exist** and **better after**: define
  photo slots that degrade elegantly (ornament, illustration, typography,
  texture — never an empty "PHOTO" box) and upgrade in place when real
  photography arrives (portrait A4 cover hero; landscape 3:2 feature slots).
- QR codes and map links are generated by production tooling — design a
  placeholder frame, never a real QR.

## Explicitly open (please explore)

- Page grid and asymmetry; margin systems; multi-column vs single-column rhythm.
- Chapter/section systems beyond numbered chapters.
- Illustration language (line art, engraving style, abstract Lebanese landscape
  motifs) as the photo-free visual layer.
- The cover concept (typographic, illustrated, monogram, photographic-when-ready).
- How dark (midnight) and light pages alternate through the book.
- The balance between editorial storytelling and instant-reference utility —
  the defining tension of this object.
