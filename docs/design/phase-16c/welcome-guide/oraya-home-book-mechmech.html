House Expectations

Please read and respect the following during your stay.

1
Visitors & Gatherings

You may host up to 20 visitors during your stay. For larger events or celebrations — catering, decoration, music, and ambience — Oraya can assist on request.

2
Restricted Areas

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, security systems, security cameras, and storage rooms are restricted. Do not access, adjust, or tamper with them.

3
Smoking

Smoking is allowed. Please smoke responsibly and take care of the property.

4
Pool Safety

No diving in the pool. Children and guests must exercise care around the pool area at all times.

5
Children & Supervision

Children and underage guests must be supervised by an adult at all times.

6
Property Care
