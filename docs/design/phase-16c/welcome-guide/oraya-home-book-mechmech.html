Kitchen

Villa kitchen

Fully equipped: gas cooktop, oven, microwave, kettle, and full cutlery and glassware. The outdoor kitchenette in the winter room also has a sink and counter. Clean all appliances and surfaces after use, return items to their place, and dispose of food waste in the outdoor bins.

BBQ

Outdoor BBQ

Outdoor BBQ outside the winter glass room, covered and sheltered. Barbecue kit included. Open the gas valve to ignite and light as labeled. After use: fully close the gas valve, allow to cool, then replace the cover. Do not leave the BBQ unattended while lit.

Bathrooms

Concept image
Concept image

Towels and toiletries are provided. Hot water is ready — see page 2 for switch details. Used towels can be left on the bathroom floor.

Garbage

Every room has a small garbage bin. Two large garbage cans are outside. Place all garbage in the outdoor cans — not on the street.

Pool & Pool Cover

Concept image

Pool is maintained before your stay. Remove the cover before swimming and replace when finished. If the pool is heated, replacing the cover when not in use retains warmth. No diving. Contact Oraya for any pool issue before attempting a reset.

Restricted Areas

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, security systems, security cameras, and storage rooms are restricted. Do not access, adjust, or tamper with them.

Restricted Areas
